Frequently asked questions

Is there a Souqi mobile app? ⌄
Yes! Souqi Mobile is available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play Store), letting you vibe code and deploy full web applications right from your phone.
What can I do with the Souqi app? ⌄
You can prompt Souqi Agent, preview live web apps instantly, edit code, manage databases, view build history, and share your creations with a single tap.
How do I install the Souqi mobile app? ⌄
Search for "Souqi" on the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, or tap the download buttons on this page.
Can I build native mobile apps with Souqi Agent? ⌄
You can build web apps directly on mobile, and to build native iOS/Android apps with Expo Go and App Store submission, open Souqi on the web.
